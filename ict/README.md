# The ICT Setup That Changed My Trading

A 50 s vertical TikTok (1080×1920, 30 fps) explaining the ICT / Smart Money model:
liquidity sweep → market structure shift → fair value gap entry.

All visuals, music and sound effects are generated by the code in this folder: the frames are drawn
procedurally on an HTML canvas (3D candlesticks, the trader room, the vault, the outro icons) and
rendered with headless Chromium. No screenshots, logos, brand names, real people or copyrighted
characters. The voice is a neural text-to-speech voice (Kokoro-82M, `am_puck`).

## The chart

19 candles, built so every step is textbook-correct: equal highs at 136 (candles 1 and 5), equal lows
at exactly 100 (candles 3 and 7), a swing high at 111 (candle 8), the sweep candle wicks to 96.5 and
closes back at 102, candle 12 closes at 117 above 111 (MSS), the FVG is candle 11's high (108) to
candle 13's low (112), entry at its midpoint 110, the retrace dips to 109.5, stop at 95.5 under the
sweep, target the equal highs at 136 (about 1:1.8). Colors: green #00FF88, red #FF3B5C, cyan #00E5FF
for liquidity, gold #FFC300 for the FVG.

## Regenerate

```sh
pip install numpy scipy pillow soundfile playwright kokoro-onnx onnx
# voice model: Kokoro-82M fp32 ONNX (npm kokoro-fp32{a,b,c}-shards, concatenated in order) and the
# am_* voice vectors from the kokoro-js npm package, packed into voices-male.npz
python3 ict/tools/patch_kokoro.py kokoro-fp32.onnx MODELS/kokoro-fp32-timed.onnx
python3 ict/audio/tts.py MODELS            # voice + word timings -> data/timeline.json
python3 ict/tools/cues.py                  # animation and sound cues -> data/cues.json
python3 ict/audio/music.py                 # 140 BPM trap, drop on scene 4
python3 ict/audio/mix.py                   # sfx + ducking + loudness -> out/audio/soundtrack.wav
python3 ict/tools/text_deliverables.py     # voiceover.txt, subtitles.srt, caption.txt
python3 ict/build.py                       # -> ict/ict.html (seek(t) draws the frame at t)
python3 ict/render/frames.py video ict/out/ict-setup-tiktok.mp4 --sub 6 --audio ict/out/audio/soundtrack.wav
```

`ict/render/frames.py stills DIR T...` renders single frames, `sheet` builds contact sheets,
`--clean` hides the captions.

## Notes

- Speech rate: the script is 137 words. Read at the voice's natural speed with short pauses, it runs
  at about 167 words per minute. At 160 wpm it would need 51 s with no pauses at all, so it could not fit 50 s.
- Emphasis on SWEEP, SHIFT, GAP, ENTRY and TARGET is a +2.5 dB lift on those words. The pauses before
  each "Step" are 0.5 s.
