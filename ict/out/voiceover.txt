Ninety percent of traders lose money. Here's the setup the other ten percent use. Smart money can't buy millions without sellers. So where do they find them? Equal lows. Retail traders hide their stop losses right below them. That's sell-side liquidity. Step one: the sweep. Price dives under the lows, triggers every stop, and snaps right back. Step two: the shift. Strong bullish candles break the last swing high. The trend just flipped. Step three: the gap. Candle one's high and candle three's low don't overlap. That empty space is your Fair Value Gap. Now be patient. Let price come back into the gap. That's your entry. Stop loss below the sweep. Target the equal highs above, where the next liquidity sits. Target hit. Clean, simple, repeatable.
